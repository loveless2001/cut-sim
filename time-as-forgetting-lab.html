Entanglement Dynamics and Candidate Entropic Clocks ASMCONJ

For a chosen quantum cut, the reduced entropy SA = −Tr(ρA log ρA) may change. The framework conditionally interprets a sufficiently monotone segment as a candidate ordering variable, τAent — “forgetting” as a model-dependent metaphor, not a universal definition of time.

The browser simulates a free-fermion chain prepared in a Néel state and released. It uses exact analytic eigenmodes and correlation-matrix evolution, the same method as the research code. The movable cut defines the left subsystem whose entanglement entropy is plotted.

The displayed t is the simulator's model parameter. It is not an observer's clock reading. A physical clock would require a declared reference, read/count procedure, and calibration rule.

What to notice

1 · Purity is not stationarity. The global state remains pure, so SU = 0 throughout this unitary model. That does not mean the state is stationary or that no internal relation changes; it means only that global von Neumann entropy cannot serve as a varying indicator here. The reduced entropy SA does vary for the chosen cut.

2 · Move the cut, change the candidate order. Drag the cut position: a different bipartition gives a different SA(t). On a monotone interval, each curve may define a different τAent. It supplies neither seconds nor a universal clock law. The current realization also does not supply a preferred cut; that scoped gap is discussed in the operational core.

3 · Recurrences limit the clock interpretation. GAP At N = 16, SA(t) rises, fluctuates, and partially revives. Unitarity gives reversible evolution; time-reversal symmetry is a separate condition. The recurrence shows that SA is not a universal monotone clock. A macroscopic arrow needs additional boundary/preparation, environmental, coarse-graining, and record-maintenance assumptions. The Orientation Lab tests a separate equivalence in a declared two-sided setup.

Physics: open-boundary tight-binding chain, quench from the Néel state; SA from the spectrum of the reduced correlation matrix. Identical numerical method to src/lattice-model-exact-fft-evolution.py, ported to JS. Interpretive scope: docs/operational-core.md and docs/cut_spec.tex v0.4.
