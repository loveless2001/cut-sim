The Cut — interactive guide

One model is shown through two access scopes. Model view includes the full state, substrate coordinates, simulator parameter, and debug quantities. Observer view includes only records supplied by the declared apparatus. The toggle illustrates this difference; Model view is the full declared realization, not a metaphysically privileged view. Model-level quantities may become accessible under expanded procedures. The simulations use the repository's free-fermion methods, and Level 5 uses measured data from the preregistered experiment.

Scope. This guide presents one quantum realization. It is not an experiment on the operational core or evidence for an ontology. Arrow conditions and cut selection remain unresolved, and one numerical claim was falsified. Each task states its procedure class and limit.

  • That entropy defines duration robustly — on this small chain it revives and fluctuates; a calibrated clock needs additional structure.

Level 6 — Scope comparison: clocks and islands

Analogy only: this level compares the need to declare a subsystem, observable, physical procedure, and inference scope CONJ. Relational-clock and island calculations use different physical assumptions and neither is derived here.

The problem of time

Some constrained formulations use a stationary global state while recovering relational dynamics from internal clocks. This is not implied by S_U = 0 alone. Which temporal observable is being asked for?

 

The information paradox

In the semiclassical setup, the radiation entropy calculation depends on the subsystem and generalized-entropy prescription.

 

WHAT THIS SHOWS

  • Both puzzles can be compared through the discipline of specifying the subsystem, observable, and inference scope. This is an analogy, not a claim that one move solves both.
  • In controlled gravitational models, the Page curve follows from extremizing generalized entropy over island/QES saddles; the area term and path integral are essential, independently developed inputs.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT SHOW

  • A derivation. The island rule uses gravitational path integrals; Page–Wootters time uses constraint quantization.
  • That the two problems have the same physical cause or solution.

Summary

The guide separates operational access, model assumptions, empirical constraints, and interpretations:

The named gaps remain in this realization. Level 4 did not derive a macroscopic arrow; Level 3's supplied selectors did not choose a cut. This does not prove that every possible theory has exactly two foundations. The ruler-cancellation mechanism came back partly falsified and partly supported in its declared model (Level 5).

Geometry from entanglement

Ryu–Takayanagi; Van Raamsdonk; and the island prescription (Penington; Almheiri–Engelhardt–Marolf–Maxfield).

Arrow & cones

Boltzmann and the past hypothesis (see D. Albert, Time and Chance); two-headed-arrow cosmologies (Carroll–Chen); Lieb–Robinson bounds; emergent Lorentz invariance literature.
